# Problem Statement

Many people struggle to keep track of where their daily money goes
because they don't record small, everyday expenses (food, travel, etc.)
in any structured way. This makes it hard to understand spending
patterns or identify categories where they are overspending.

## Scope of the Project
This project is a lightweight, command-line expense tracker built in
Python. It allows a user to log expenses as they occur, view a full
list of past expenses, and get a category-wise breakdown of spending.
The scope is limited to a single-session, in-memory tracker (data is
not saved to a file or database) — it is meant to demonstrate core
programming concepts such as lists, tuples, dictionaries, functions,
and loops, rather than serve as a production-ready finance app.

## Target Users
Anyone who wants a simple, no-frills way to log and review their daily
expenses — students, individuals managing personal budgets, or anyone
learning to track spending habits without needing a complex app.

## High-Level Features
- Add an expense with category, amount, and date
- View all logged expenses
- Get a category-wise spending summary with total
- Identify the highest and lowest spending categories
- Search expenses by category
